SEHATist — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

SEHATist is operated by Nutraganic Biotech (trading as SEHATist), 102, 1st Floor, Raja Garden, near Joginder Nagar, Yamunanagar, Haryana 135001, India ("we", "us"). This policy covers both the SEHATist Android app and the sehatist.com website — what each collects, why, and what happens to it.

In short

  • Your trackers — water, food, habits and streaks — stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • We receive your details in only two situations: when you ask for a plan in the app, and when you fill in the enquiry form on the website — in both cases because a person has to read them and get back to you.
  • Payments go through Razorpay. When you buy a plan you pay on Razorpay's secure page. We never see your card, UPI or bank details.
  • We never sell your data, and we never hand your name, number, email or body details to anyone to use for their own purposes.
  • We advertise SEHATist on Facebook and Instagram, and we measure whether those ads work. That measurement sends Meta a device identifier and the fact that you installed the app or visited the site — never your name, number, email, weight, food log or health answers. § 5a explains it in full, and how to switch it off.

1. What stays on your device only

Stored locally on your phone and never transmitted to us:

Data Why
Water intake, and the weight/height you enter To calculate and track your daily water goal
Foods added to the calorie calculator To total calories and macros for that day
Habit ticks and streaks To keep your streaks running
Your weekly weight log To draw your weight chart
Free health-evaluation answers To generate your on-device health report
Reminder settings To schedule notifications on your phone

This is held in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it permanently. We hold no copy, so we cannot restore it.

One exception, so this is not misleading: a few basics you type in — age, gender, weight, height, goal and dietary preference — are reused to pre-fill the plan enquiry form, so you do not have to type them twice. They stay on your phone unless you choose to submit that form. If you never submit it, they never leave your device. See § 3.

2. No health or fitness data is read from your phone

SEHATist does not read data from Android Health Connect, Google Fit, your step sensor, your location, your camera or any other sensor. It asks for no health permissions at all.

Everything the app tracks is something you typed or tapped yourself.

3. What we receive when you ask for a plan

A plan is written by a real SEHATist expert, not generated by software, so that expert needs to know about you. Only when you submit a plan enquiry do we receive:

  • Name, mobile number and email address
  • Age, gender, weight and height
  • Goal, dietary preference and activity level
  • Which plan you asked about, its listed price, and a reference number

Submitting the form does not charge you by itself. Next you are taken to Razorpay to pay for the plan (see § 4), and your Sehat Expert starts once the payment is confirmed. If you do not pay, nothing is charged, and the order is kept only as long as any other enquiry (see § 6).

We deliberately do not ask for, collect, or store any medical condition, diagnosis, medication or medical history. SEHATist offers general fitness and nutrition guidance only — it does not diagnose, treat or manage any medical condition, so we have no reason to hold that information.

This is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to a private endpoint we control. It arrives as an email in our business inbox, and is also written to a private spreadsheet only we can open, so that an enquiry cannot be lost to a spam filter. Your expert then contacts you on WhatsApp.

We do not receive your water log, food log, habits, streaks or health evaluation answers at any point.

If you fill in the "Talk to an expert" form on sehatist.com, we receive only what you type into it:

Field Required Why
Name Yes So we know who we are replying to
Mobile number Yes So an expert can call or message you

Goal (lose weight, build muscle, get toned, boost stamina) Yes So the reply is relevant

We ask for this only on the basis of your consent, which you give by ticking the consent box before submitting. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us (see § 7) and we will delete the enquiry.

The form does not ask for, and you should not send, any medical condition, diagnosis, medication or medical history. SEHATist gives general fitness guidance, not treatment, so we have no reason to hold that.

The submission travels over HTTPS to a Google Apps Script endpoint we control, which emails it to our business inbox. It is not stored in any database, and duplicate submissions from the same person within six hours are discarded.

The website sets no tracking cookie until you agree to it. On your first visit you are asked. Decline and nothing is sent to Meta and no tracking cookie is set — the site works exactly as before.

The one thing we store either way is your answer, kept in your own browser so we do not ask again on every page. It is a single yes-or-no value, it never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it. If you accept, we also load the Meta Pixel, described in § 5a.

Web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request — see the processor table in § 5.

4. Payments

When you buy a plan in the app, you pay on Razorpay's secure payment page, which opens in your browser. Razorpay Software Private Limited is our payment processor.

  • We never see your card, UPI or bank details. You enter them on Razorpay's page, not in our app, and Razorpay handles them under its own security certification (PCI DSS).
  • What Razorpay receives from us: the plan, the amount, your name and mobile number, and your email if you gave one, so the payment page is filled in for you and your receipt reaches you.
  • What we receive back from Razorpay: whether the payment went through, the amount, and a payment reference. That is how we know to start your plan.
  • While an order is unpaid, the app checks its status with us now and then, sending only the order's reference number, so it can tell you once the payment is confirmed.
  • How long we keep payment records is set out in § 6.

Cancellations and refunds are covered in our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

5. Who else can see your data

We never sell your personal data. Your name, mobile number, email address, age, gender, weight, height, goal, diet, logs, streaks and health answers are never given to anyone to use for their own purposes — not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to "marketing partners". Nobody buys them from us and nobody is handed them.

One thing does go to a company that uses it for its own purposes, and we would rather say so plainly than bury it: the advertising identifier described in § 5a. Meta receives it to tell us whether our ads worked, and also uses it to improve how Meta targets advertising generally. That is genuine sharing, not processing on our behalf, and we declare it as sharing on our Google Play listing too. It is a device number, not you, and § 5a explains how to switch it off.

The app contains no ads. You will never see an advert inside SEHATist, and we do not sell advertising space. We do advertise the app elsewhere, and we measure whether that advertising works — which is a different thing, and § 5a sets out exactly what it involves.

Everything else goes to a short list of service providers, which is a different relationship: they handle data only on our instructions and may not use it for anything of their own. Both the DPDP Act and Google Play treat that as different from sharing. You should still know who they are:

5a. Advertising and measurement

We pay to show SEHATist ads on Facebook and Instagram. To know whether that money is being wasted, Meta has to be able to tell us how many people who saw an ad went on to install the app or fill in the enquiry form. That requires sending Meta a small amount of data.

In the app, we include the Meta SDK. It reports:

  • that the app was installed, and that it was opened
  • that someone began the plan enquiry form, and that someone submitted it — the fact that each happened, and nothing else. Not what you typed into it, not which plan you asked about, not the price. Meta is told a form was started, and later that one was submitted, on this device. That is what lets us tell an advertisement that brings us enquiries from one that only brings us installs.
  • your device's advertising ID — a resettable number Android gives to apps for exactly this purpose, which is not your name and which you can reset or switch off at any time
  • basic device information: model, operating system version, language

On this website, if you accept cookies, we load the Meta Pixel. It reports which pages you viewed and whether you submitted the enquiry form, and sets cookies in your browser so Meta can recognise the same browser later.

What is never sent

Your name, mobile number, email address, age, gender, weight, height, goal, dietary preference, water log, food log, habits, streaks, health-test answers and anything you type into a form. None of it. Nor which plan you enquired about, nor what it costs. Meta learns that a device installed the app and that a form was started and submitted on it — not who you are, not what you asked for, and nothing about your body.

How to stop it

  • In the app: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID. Every app on your phone, including ours, then has nothing to report.
  • On this website: decline cookies when asked, or clear this site's cookies in your browser. Declining changes nothing about how the site works.
  • Either way: email support@sehatist.com and we will exclude you.

Neither choice affects your plan, your price, or how your Sehat Expert treats you. We will never know you opted out.

Why we do it

Honestly: because we are small and advertising is how people find us. Without measurement we cannot tell a campaign that works from one that burns money, which means spending more to reach the same people. It is not used to build a profile of you, and nothing about your health is involved.

6. How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you uninstall or clear the app's data.
  • Advertising measurement data: held by Meta under their own retention rules, not ours — we cannot delete it for you, which is why the switch-offs in § 5a matter. We see only aggregate counts, never a list of people.
  • Plan enquiries, whether from the app or the website: kept for up to 12 months so we can follow up, then deleted. Ask us sooner and we will delete them. We keep no longer than this because the details go stale — a weight from two years ago is no use for writing a plan, we would ask you again anyway.
  • Once you actually pay for a plan: the commercial record of that sale — your name, the plan, the amount and the date — is kept for 8 years, because tax law requires it and we cannot shorten it. The body details you gave us to write the plan — weight, height, goal and dietary preference — are deleted after 12 months, as we have no reason to keep them.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023 you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data of yours we hold
  • correct anything inaccurate
  • delete your data
  • withdraw consent (though we may then be unable to deliver a plan you have asked us for)
  • opt out of advertising measurement — see § 5a, which you can do yourself without asking us

Email support@sehatist.com and we will respond within 30 days.

For app data on your phone, uninstalling deletes it immediately — no request needed. For a website enquiry, email us and we will delete it.

8. Children

SEHATist is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, email support@sehatist.com and we will delete it.

9. Security

All transmission uses HTTPS. On-device data sits in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Not medical advice

SEHATist provides general fitness and nutrition guidance only. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or manage any medical condition, and it is not a substitute for professional medical care.

The health score, BMI and calorie figures are rough estimates for guidance, not clinical measurements. Always consult a qualified doctor before changing your diet or exercise, particularly if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or take medication.
